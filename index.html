Movie Night

Paste the Google Drive sharing link to watch the movie here.

Set it up

  1. Upload the 3–4 GB movie to Google Drive and wait for the upload to finish processing.
  2. On Drive, open the file’s Share settings and set General access to Anyone with the link — Viewer.
  3. Copy the file’s sharing link, paste it above, and press Watch movie.
  4. Send your girlfriend this website’s URL. She pastes the same Drive link to play it.
